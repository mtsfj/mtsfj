### About

I work on the **automatic discovery of LLM failure modes**: curious agents that set their own goals, then map what a large generative model can do and where it breaks.

- PhD student in the **Flowers** team at **Inria Bordeaux** (2026 – 2029), advised by Pierre-Yves Oudeyer.
- Before: M.Sc. CS at **Georgia Tech**, engineering at **IMT Atlantique**, research with the **Schölkopf group** (MPI-IS / ELLIS Tübingen), internships at **Thales** and **Airbus**.
